# States and Motion

- **Buttons:** primary red → Crimson on hover → Pepper pressed; outlined secondaries fill on hover (red, or blue for `secondary-alt`); inverted buttons lighten. Disabled is Rhino with Asphalt text on light grounds, 50% opacity on dark.
- **Text links:** the arrow nudges forward 4px on hover and press; red and blue links darken. Disabled is 50% opacity. A breadcrumb link shows a 2px bar under its label on hover and darkens when pressed.
- **Tabs, menus and list rows share one rule.** Hover is neutral: the row takes a 5% Charcoal tint (`chip-bg`), and a tab label darkens to `text-primary`. Active is blue: the row sits on Misty Blue (`surface-blue-200`) with the blue marker on its left edge; the active tab is Qualys Blue over its blue indicator. This holds for the navigation menu's rail, the list AccordionModule and Tabs, and for this site's own menu. Hover never looks like active.
- **Cards:** the whole card is the link; hover slides a 6px `qualys-blue` bar in from left to right along the bottom edge and nudges the arrow: forward for a text-link arrow, up and to the right for the outlink icon. The shadow stays as it is. No scaling or bouncing.
- **Focus:** `focus-ring` — a 2px white gap then a solid 2px Qualys Blue ring — on buttons, links, cards, checkboxes, radios and toggles; text fields, dropdowns and search bars show a Qualys Blue border instead.
- **Timing:** 150ms for colour, 200ms for buttons, inputs and cards, 300ms for larger movements such as the card bar, the sliding tab and navigation indicators, and the navigation menus sliding open (from under the bar on desktop, from the right edge on mobile); ease-in-out. Entrances fade in. Drawers animate their height (300ms) and turn the chevron. The Marker in an accordion list slides to the open row (300ms). Stat figures count up from 0 once, when they scroll into view (1.2s, easing out).
- **Overlays and dialogs:** the backdrop fades in over 200ms and the modal fades in with it, growing from 95%: the one place something scales. Toasts fade in over 300ms (rising 12px in a stack), the cookie bar rises over 400ms, tooltips fade over 150ms after a 300ms wait (at once on keyboard focus). A hover card opens 150ms after the pointer arrives, stays while the pointer is on it or its trigger and leaves 200ms after; a click, tap or Enter keeps it open until it is closed. Esc closes whatever can be dismissed; alert dialogs, the session warning and cookie consent wait for a button.
- **Toasts** leave by themselves: success and info after 5 seconds, warnings after 8; errors stay until dismissed. The timer pauses under the pointer.
- **Carousels** move only when asked: by swipe, arrow keys or their previous / next buttons, one card at a time, with the progress bar following. The logo carousel moves by itself; it pauses on hover, on focus, while a hover card is open and with its pause button. Tabs may autoplay (six seconds a tab) only when asked to: they wait on hover and focus, stop for good once the reader picks a tab, and carry a pause button.
- **Reduced motion:** when someone has asked their device for less motion, menus, tabs and bars change without sliding, modals, toasts and the cookie bar appear without fading or moving, the logo carousel stays still, tabs do not autoplay, stat figures show their final number and background video doesn't autoplay.

# Content

**Personality:** reliable, calm, confident, trustworthy, warm, approachable. Write for security professionals and enterprise buyers: precise and jargon-aware, never intimidating or overly technical in marketing copy.

- **Address the reader.** Second person — "you", "your team", "your environment". Use "we" sparingly, for company context only, never in product descriptions.
- **Casing.** Title Case for headings, display text, buttons, tab names and CTAs — never all caps for headings. Sentence case for body copy, UI text, descriptions and labels under stats. All caps only for short labels: tags and eyebrows.
- **Length.** Headings under ten words. Supporting copy one to three sentences. No padding.
- **CTAs** start with an action verb and are never passive: "Get a Demo", "Get Started", "Learn More", "Register Now", "Explore the Platform", "Download Report".
- **Numbers.** Spell out one to nine in running copy; numerals for stats, data and UI labels ("3,847 patched").
- **Punctuation.** Oxford comma. Em dashes for emphasis — not hyphens.
- **Dates and times** (Figma Typography page): long date "Mon Dec 9, 2024" for headers, detail pages and featured content; short date "Dec 9, 2024" on compact cards; date and time range "Mon Dec 9, 2024  10:00 – 11:00a PST" for events; time range alone "8:00 AM – 12:00 PM" when the date is already clear. Event cards join the short date and start time with a bullet: "Dec 10, 2026 • 11a PST". Set dates and times in Medium (500).
- **No emoji** in product or marketing copy.

Examples in the house voice:

> **Detect, Prioritize, and Eliminate Risk**
> The Qualys Cloud Platform gives your team a single source of truth across your entire hybrid IT environment.
> [Get a Demo] [Explore the Platform]

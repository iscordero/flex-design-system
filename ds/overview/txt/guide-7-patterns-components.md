# Patterns and Components

## Patterns

Rules from the Qualys review rounds and the Figma pages for recurring patterns:

- **Avatars / profile cards:** small (40px) and medium (56px) avatars are circles, large (300px) is a square; no XL size. A missing photo shows the default avatar, never a broken image or a gap. Profile cards carry a LinkedIn SocialButton on the photo and sit three across (or five in the combined container); names in the title styles with a tight gap to the designation (`paragraph-rg`); a card with a bio shows "Read Bio" on hover and opens a modal over a 70% black backdrop. Session speakers inside agenda cards follow DrawerCard.
- **Navigation and footer:** Nav is the global bar on every page: logo, up to six primary items, utility links, search and one primary call to action. "Platform"-style items open a mega menu (overview, a filter list and groups of links); smaller sets open a column dropdown. Below 1200px it becomes a menu button and a full-screen, drill-down menu. NavBanner sits above it for one event promotion at a time. Breadcrumbs sit under the Nav, above the page's first module, on pages two or more levels deep: a link for each level and the current page as text; on mobile only the level above and the page show. Footer closes every page: the shield in its notch, a short pitch with the URL form, social links, three link columns and the legal line.
- **Block modules:** a page is a stack of full-width modules on the 1140px column. Start with one Hero (split with media, text only, on a photo or video, or with a lead form); use TypographicHeader to open a section with words alone; GeneralContent for copy beside media, media first or last, or as a full-width banner; TabModule when one area of the page has several views; AccordionModule for detail people open a row at a time, on its own, beside copy or media, or as a list with the open row beside it; MarketingTable to set a standard or a list of requirements beside what Qualys offers, a row at a time, with the Qualys column featured; FeatureMatrix or DetailComparison to compare Qualys with competitors; FormBanner for a lead form with its own headline; CtaModule to close with a form or a single call to action. One primary call to action per module, and one `h1` per page (the Hero's).
- **Stats sections:** Stats shows three figures in one panel or three to four as cards; four in a row use `stat-md`. Keep each figure to a number, a short label and one line of support.
- **Carousels:** use Carousel for four or more cards of one kind; three or fewer go in a grid. It never moves by itself. TestimonialCarousel is the same pattern for customer quotes (large, on a photo, small, or with a 9:16 video); once per page.
- **Logos:** LogoCarousel for a moving row of customer logos (five or more) with a pause button; LogoHierarchy for sponsors or partners in tiers, highest first. Both use the companies' own logo files.
- **Events calendar:** EventCalendar shows the same events as a month grid, a day-by-day list or a grid of cards, under one search and filter bar. In the month, bars sit under the date and a day with several events shows compact bars with just the name; event types keep their colours and icons from EventType.
- **Modals and dialogs:** Modal is the base (title, close, body, buttons on the right with the primary last). Use AlertDialog when a decision must be made before anything else, and name the action on its button; SuccessConfirmation after an action that deserves a stop; FormModal for a lead form; DemoRequestWizard when the form needs two short steps; ExitIntent once per visit at most; SessionTimeout five minutes before sign-out. One modal at a time, never one over another. Everything closes with Esc and the backdrop except alert dialogs, the session warning and cookie consent.
- **Hover cards:** a HoverCard explains a logo or a headshot with a few lines and one link: the logo card (logo, badge, name, copy, TextLink) and the profile card (avatar, full name, role, with the LinkedIn icon and the close icon together at the top right). Use a Tooltip for a few words of plain text, a HoverCard when there is a link, a modal when there is more to read.
- **Comparison tables:** FeatureMatrix for yes/no feature coverage against competitors, DetailComparison when each difference needs a sentence, MarketingTable to set requirements beside what Qualys offers, with the Qualys column featured. In the two competitor tables Qualys is always the first, highlighted column. On mobile the matrix and the detail table compare one competitor at a time. Claims about competitors must be current and checked.
- **Notifications:** pick by how much it should interrupt. Toast (through ToastStack) for the result of something the person just did; NotificationBar for news that concerns the whole site, above the Nav, one at a time; Tooltip for a few words about one control; a dialog only when the page can't continue without an answer. Status is always a colour and an icon together.
- **Cookie consent:** CookieBanner's compact bar on the first visit, its panel from "Manage Preferences" and from the footer link. It stays until answered.
- **Forms:** white 56px fields whose label floats up when focused or filled (TextField, Select), stacked `spacing-rg` apart, with one primary Button. Errors show a red border and a 14px red message under the field. Checkbox, Radio and Toggle for choices. Form is the ready-made lead-capture card in four variants: `full` for trials and demos, `extended` when the offer needs qualifying questions, `gate` for gated content and `minimal` for a single field. Each has a heading, the fields, an optional opt-in, one button and the consent line; on mobile the fields stack and the button spans the width.
- **Filters:** use them above large lists, tables, galleries and catalogues that people narrow by several attributes; skip them under about ten items or when results wouldn't change. Two patterns: a **filter cloud** (FilterChipGroup) that shows every filter as a toggle chip with its result count, and **filter dropdowns** (FilterBar) with checkboxes and a "Reset Filters" link, five categories at most. On mobile both move into FilterDrawer, where choices apply together on "Apply Filters": the cloud becomes a FilterButton beside the search with AppliedFilters (removable chips, "+N more" and the result count) under it; the dropdowns become one "Filter" control. Chip labels are one to three words; counts over 999 show as "1k+"; a chip with no results is disabled.

## Components

`window.Qualys` (`components/bundle.js`, React 18) provides everything below, in three levels. Each one's README gives what to pass and its rules. On this page they are grouped by family, so a component sits with the patterns and modules built from it (Form, then FormModal; Toast, then ToastStack). Families run A to Z, and so do the components inside each one. Each card's subtitle starts with its level.

- **Components** are the single parts, used inside patterns and modules or on their own: Button, TextLink, Hyperlink, Breadcrumbs, SocialButton, Icon, Tag, FilterChip, Flag, Marker, EventType, TextField, Select, SearchBar, Checkbox, Radio, Toggle, FilterDropdown, FilterButton, Tabs, Drawer, DrawerCard, Accordion, Avatar, ProfileCard, ProfileModal, HoverCard, Card, IntegrationCard, EventCard, WebinarCard, ResourceCard, BlogCard, InlinePromoCard, StatCard, TestimonialCard, Media, Tooltip, Overlay, Modal, AlertDialog and Toast.
- **Patterns** combine components for one recurring task: Form (lead capture), FilterBar, FilterChipGroup, AppliedFilters and FilterDrawer (filtering a list), ProfileGrid (a team or speaker line-up), ToastStack (placing and timing toasts), FormModal, DemoRequestWizard, ExitIntent, SuccessConfirmation and SessionTimeout (the modal flows), and CookieBanner (consent).
- **Block modules** are full-width page sections on the 1140px column, stacked to build a page: Nav, NavBanner, NotificationBar, Hero, TypographicHeader, GeneralContent, TabModule, AccordionModule, CtaModule, Stats, Carousel, TestimonialCarousel, LogoCarousel, LogoHierarchy, EventCalendar, FormBanner, FeatureMatrix, DetailComparison, MarketingTable and Footer.

Build upward: reach for a block module first, a pattern when part of a page needs one, and single components to fill what is left.

`components/bundle.css` adds the `.q-container` / `.q-grid` layout classes and the mobile type and spacing values. Load `bundle.css` after the tokens, and the Google Fonts for Wix Madefor Text and IBM Plex Sans.

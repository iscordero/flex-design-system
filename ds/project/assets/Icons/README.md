# Icons

Utility icons, single ink in `currentColor`. In an `<img>` they render black; inline them (or use the `Icon` component) to colour them with CSS — `icon-default` on light grounds, `icon-inverted` on dark, `icon-feature` or `qualys-blue` for interactive.

- From the Figma Buttons sheet (16 × 16): `icon-download`, `icon-arrow-right`; `icon-arrow-left` and `icon-outlink` are that arrow rotated 180° and −45°.
- From the Figma Inputs sheet (16 × 16): `icon-search`, `icon-check` and `icon-chevron-down`; `icon-chevron-up`, `-left` and `-right` are that chevron rotated.
- From the Figma Cards file (24 × 24): `icon-time`, the clock beside durations and reading times on webinar and blog cards.
- From the Figma Filters page (24 × 24): `icon-filter`, the three lines beside "Filter" on mobile.
- From the Figma Navigation page (20 × 16): `icon-menu`, the menu button on the mobile bar.
- From the Figma Calendar page (20 × 20): `icon-view-list`, `icon-view-month` and `icon-view-grid`, the three buttons of the calendar's view toggle.
- From the Figma Modals, Popups & Tooltips file: `icon-alert-triangle` (32 × 32), `icon-alert-circle`, `icon-check-circle`, `icon-x-circle` and `icon-file` (24 × 24) are line icons, 2 to 3px strokes with round ends; `icon-cookie` (24 × 24) and `icon-party` (16 × 16) are solid. They carry status in dialogs, toasts and the notification bar, always in that status's colour.
- From the code kit's set (24 × 24, centred): `icon-cross`, `icon-plus`.
- 16px in buttons, text links and fields, 4px from a button label, 8px from field text.
